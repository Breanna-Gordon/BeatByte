Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions from creators, developers and collaborators using BeatByte.

BeatByte is a platform designed to support ethical collaboration in game audio. We connect music creators and game developers through licensed assets, creator tools and engine integrations.

BeatByte is built for game developers who need legally secure, production ready audio, and music creators who want sustainable, paid work in interactive media.

All audio on BeatByte includes clear licensing terms designed for game development. Developers can use music confidently while creators are properly credited and compensated.

Creator Tools help composers and sound designers structure, prepare and export audio specifically for interactive game environments.

BeatByte provides integrations for Unity and Unreal, with workflows designed to reduce implementation friction.

Of Course! BeatByte supports teams of all sizes, including solo creators and indie developers who need accessible tools and transparent licensing.

Pricing varies based on licensing type and project scope. We offer transparent pricing tiers for both individual tracks and custom commissions, with options suitable for indie budgets and larger studios.

Creators can sign up, create a profile and start uploading their music. Our Creator Tools guide you through preparing audio for game engines and you'll have access to commission opportunities from developers.

Developers can browse our audio library, preview tracks and license music with clear terms using our engine plugins. You can also post commission requests to work directly with composers on custom audio.

Unlike generic stock libraries, BeatByte focuses specifically on game audio with fair creator compensation and tools built for game development workflows.
