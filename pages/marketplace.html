Explore the Marketplace

What the Marketplace Does

BeatByte Marketplace is the core service of the platform which provides a dedicated space for licensing and commissioning game audio.

Participation in the marketplace is supported through project based licensing alongside optional platform services, including:

Enhanced creator accounts

Creator accounts offering increased visibility, analytics and promotional support within the platform

Developer workflows

Subscription based access to collaboration tools, licensing management and engine integration support

Custom licensing

Flexible licensing arrangements and talent sourcing for mid-sized and enterprise level studios

What’s Available

Game-ready music & sound assets

Curated music packs and sound assets designed specifically for interactive environments. This includes looping structures and adaptive layers.

Custom soundtrack commissions

Developers can commission original music directly from creators through contracts, timelines and licensing terms managed through the platform.

Tiered licensing

Clear licensing tiers for indie, commercial and enterprise projects, ensuring usage rights scale appropriately

Who the Marketplace Is For

Designed primarily for game studios that require legally secure, production ready audio without the overhead of traditional studio commissioning models.

At the same time, we provide music creators with paid access to professional game development work and support sustainable creative careers beyond unpaid labour.
