Our Mission

Building a Fair Ecosystem for Game Audio

Game developers often struggle to source affordable, legally sound music, while musicians face unstable income. BeatByte bridges this gap by providing a dedicated space where creators and developers can connect directly.

Our Core Values

Transparency

Clear licensing terms and visible revenue splits. We believe in total clarity for every transaction.

Fair Compensation

Rejecting speculative or unpaid work. We ensure creative labor is valued and paid as professional work.

Collaboration

Structured interaction between artists and developers, moving beyond the "stock library" model.

Sustainability

Building long-term creative infrastructure rather than disposable content for the digital economy.

Value for Developers

  • Legal clarity you can trust

    Every track comes with straightforward licensing terms so you know exactly what you can use

  • Built for game development

    Music designed for interactive gameplay

  • Flexible commissioning

    Work directly with composers on cutom pieces that fit your timeline and budget

Value for Producers

  • Real opportunities, fair pay

    Access paid commissions from active game studios

  • No hidden terms

    Transparent contracts that clearly outline usage rights and compensation

  • Tools that match your workflow

    Resources for preparing audio that works in game engines

BeatByte's Social Impact

Unlike stock libraries or freelance platforms, BeatByte embeds ethical labor practices into its design. Clear contracts, active moderation and pricing standards reduce exploitation and unpaid labor common in creative technology industries. We believe fair compensation isn't optional; it's foundational. We prioritise accessibility by building a space where emerging composers can gain experience while developers can access high quality audio. This creates opportunities on both sides that traditional industry gatekeeping often prevents.

By putting fairness first, we're building a sustainable creative economy that works for everyone.
