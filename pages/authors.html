About the Authors

This organisation was developed collaboratively by our team combining interests in game development, music technology and digital platforms.

Katie

Creating BeatByte allowed me to explore how computer science can address real world industry challenges. BeatByte represents the intersection of my values and interests in computer science. As someone interested in creativity, fairness and collaboration, designing a platform that embeds these principles felt meaningful. Throughout this project, I found it fun to design the platform's architecture and structing the business model to prioritise transparency over exploitation. In addition, I enjoyed seeing how technical decisions e.g, licensing frameworks and engine integrations, directly impact people's creative freedom. This process reinforced my interest in pursuing a career that aligns with my values. I aim to work in fields where technology serves creative communities, whether in audio technology, platform development or digital creative tools. This project taught me the importance of designing ethical, accessible platforms that make creative creative collaboration sustainable and fair for everyone.

Breanna

Creating BeatByte helped me explore how computer science can be applied to real problems within creative industries. This project connected closely to my interest in fairness, transparency, and accessibility, particularly in audio media where creative workers are often undervalued or taken advantage of by unfair contracts and distribution practices. Planning the platform gave me the opportunity to think about how better working conditions for creators could be supported. Throughout the project, I focused on how the proposed platform structure, licensing decisions, and collaboration processes would affect users. Considering how these choices influence accessibility and sustainability helped me understand the wider impact of software beyond its technical function. As a Computer Science student, my goal is to hopefully be able to work in areas where technology is used thoughtfully and responsibly. I am especially interested in designing platforms and tools that are able to support a range of communities in the future. BeatByte reflects the kind of work I hope to pursue in my future creating clear, reliable systems that support users fairly, particularly those who may have less access to opportunities or resources.

Katy

My key achievement in this project was the implementation of a high-performance, interactive UI that balances complex user needs with ethical labor practices. Through this process, I gained significant expertise in full-stack development and real-time data visualization. More importantly, I learned how to transform qualitative creative requirements into scalable technical architectures. This project has sharpened my ability to leverage data-driven insights to build more equitable and efficient creative ecosystems—a core competency I intend to carry forward into my professional engineering career.
